§ 01

The algorithm, in four moves

  1. Step 01 / Scatter

    Start with raw points

    No labels, no answers. K-means only knows where each point is, and how far apart any two points are.

  2. Step 02 / Seed

    Drop K centroids

    Pick K starting centres at random. They are guesses: one candidate centre for each cluster we hope to find.

  3. Step 03 / Assign

    Every point picks its nearest centre

    Measure the straight-line distance from each point to every centroid. The closest one wins. That is the whole rule.

  4. Step 04 / Update

    Move centres to the mean, repeat

    Each centroid moves to the average of the points that chose it. Then assign again. Stop when nothing changes.

§ 03

Inertia per iteration

Inertia is the sum of squared distances from each point to its centroid. Every step of K-means can only lower it, or leave it alone.

§ 04

The elbow

Run K = 1 to 8 on the same data. Inertia always falls as K grows, so look for the bend where adding another cluster stops buying much.
